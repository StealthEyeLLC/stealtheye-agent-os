# AI Governance

AI governance covers authority, risk, evals, evidence, oversight, and continuous improvement.

## Controls

- Mission-scoped authority envelopes.
- Guard and Semantic Firewall enforcement.
- Evals for safety, task success, and regression.
- Receipts for traceability and replay.
- Risk register and control matrix.
- Human escalation for hard stops.

## No certification claim

This repo documents readiness intent and control design; it does not claim ISO, SOC 2, EU AI Act, or other certification.
