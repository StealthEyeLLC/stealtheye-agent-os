# Receipts

Receipts are the evidence ledger for autonomous work.

## Receipt schema concept

A receipt should include mission id, actor/agent identity, timestamp, authority reference, tool/action type, inputs summary, outputs summary, artifacts, policy decisions, redactions, errors, and follow-up obligations.

## Mission timeline

The ledger should reconstruct mission planning, approvals, tool calls, denied actions, diffs, logs, screenshots, artifacts, CI results, browser QA evidence, and final reports.

## Replay model

Replay should answer what happened, why it was authorized, what changed, what evidence supports the result, what was denied, and what remains incomplete. Replay does not require exposing secrets or raw sensitive data.
